Privacy

      
Browsing history is not stored persistently. Recently closed tabs remain in memory; cookies, cache, and site storage may persist until you clear them. Nacre telemetry is not configured to be sent.

      
Built-in protections block a list of known tracking domains, filter identifiable third-party cookies, and send the Global Privacy Control signal. They cannot hide your identity from a site where you sign in or replace regular updates to a browser maintained at large scale.
